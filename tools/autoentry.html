AutoEntry

Intelligent document scanning for receipts, bills, and paper bank statements.

Editorial Analysis

The most cost-flexible document extraction tool for bookkeepers due to its credit-based pay-as-you-go structure.

✓ Core UK Strengths
  • Bank Statement OCR
  • Line Item Extraction
  • Cross-accounting Sync
⚠️ Key Limitations

Interface is utility-driven and less modern than Dext, though functionally very solid.

💡 Who Should Skip

Founders who want a glossy mobile app experience rather than a high-volume desktop processing queue.
