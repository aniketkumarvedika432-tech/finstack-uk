FreeAgent

Intuitive accounting software free for NatWest/RBS business account holders.

Editorial Analysis

The most stress-free accounting software for solo freelancers. Unbeatable value if you bank with NatWest, RBS, or Mettle since it is included for free.

✓ Core UK Strengths
  • Self Assessment Filing
  • Corporation Tax
  • Free with banking partners
  • Time Tracking
⚠️ Key Limitations

Less flexible for complex inventory management or mid-market companies with complex departmental cost centres.

💡 Who Should Skip

Larger limited companies with multi-warehouse inventory or multi-tier approval workflows.
