About

Financial software discovery, made simpler.

FinStack is an independent directory concept focused on helping UK SMEs discover and compare business finance software.

What FinStack is

FinStack organises fintech and business software by practical SME needs, with a focus on UK relevance.

What FinStack is not

It is not a bank, lender, financial adviser, tax adviser or law firm. Users should verify product terms and suitability directly with providers.
