Editorial Standards

How We Evaluate & Score Financial Tools

FinStack UK uses an independent, multi-factor benchmarking framework specifically tailored to British small and medium-sized enterprises (SMEs).

1. The Editorial Scoring Framework (1.0 – 5.0★)

Every rating published across our directory represents a weighted composite benchmark. Rather than relying solely on arbitrary editorial opinions or unverified on-site reviews, each score is normalized across three core evaluation pillars:

A. Public Industry Sentiment (40% Weighting)

We aggregate verified business customer reviews across trusted independent platforms (including Trustpilot UK, G2, Capterra, and official mobile App Stores) to evaluate long-term reliability and customer support.

B. UK Regulatory & Workflow Compatibility (35% Weighting)

Software is scrutinized for native British compliance readiness, including HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD) certification, RTI payroll submission capabilities, FSCS deposit protection, CIS handling, and Companies House filing integrations.

C. Pricing Transparency & Value for Money (25% Weighting)

We reward transparent monthly pricing, low transaction surcharges, and accessible free or micro-business tiers over restrictive annual lock-ins and opaque fee structures.

2. Editorial Independence & Commercial Transparency

FinStack UK maintains complete editorial control over all tool inclusions, descriptions, and ratings.

Some links on our platform may generate partner compensation at no additional cost to our users. However, commercial partnerships never dictate a provider's placement, feature breakdown, or composite rating. Providers cannot purchase higher scores or alter our compliance evaluation criteria.

3. Disclaimer & Regular Audits

FinStack is an independent software discovery directory and does not provide financial, legal, or tax advice. We conduct periodic data audits to maintain accurate pricing and feature snapshots as UK provider terms evolve.
