Data Integrity & Standards

Our Curation & Sourcing Standards

To maintain editorial integrity, every software listing on FinStack is evaluated using verified public review aggregators and direct statutory compliance audits[cite: 7].

1. Public Review Data Sourcing

We do not invent proprietary review scores[cite: 7]. Every rating displayed across our cards and comparison tables is mapped to an audited public source[cite: 4, 7]:

A. Trustpilot UK (Consumer & Commercial Accounts)[cite: 7]

Used primarily for UK digital banks, neo-banks, lending products, and SME bookkeeping software[cite: 4, 7].

B. G2 & Capterra (B2B Infrastructure & Developer Gateways)[cite: 7]

Used for enterprise spend management tools, developer payment APIs (e.g., Stripe), and multi-currency billing engines[cite: 4, 7].

C. Timestamp Audits[cite: 7]

All scores display their audit date (Audited August 2026) to prevent stale or manipulated metrics[cite: 4, 7].

2. UK Compliance Criteria

Beyond sentiment, products must satisfy clear UK compliance benchmarks[cite: 7]:

  • HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD): Confirmation of functional digital VAT submission APIs[cite: 7].
  • FSCS Banking Safeguards: Verification of £85,000 FSCS deposit insurance versus FCA ring-fenced safeguarding accounts[cite: 1, 4, 7].
  • PAYE & Auto-Enrolment: Support for direct HMRC RTI submissions and The Pensions Regulator compliance[cite: 4, 7].

3. Commercial Disclosure

FinStack UK is entirely free for businesses to use[cite: 1, 7]. We may receive referral compensation from selected software providers via affiliate partnerships[cite: 4, 7]. This compensation does not alter review scores, verified drawback notes, or tool ranking order[cite: 7].
