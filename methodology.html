Data Integrity & Standards

How We Verify & Score Financial Tools

FinStack UK uses direct, timestamped ratings sourced from independent B2B review aggregators combined with objective UK regulatory compliance checks.

1. Transparent Public Review Sourcing

To ensure total transparency and eliminate biased editorial scoring, every rating published on FinStack is mapped directly to a verified public source:

A. Trustpilot UK (Consumer & Business Feedback)

For UK business banking, lending, and SME accounting tools, ratings and review volumes reflect live Trustpilot UK scores.

B. G2 & Capterra (B2B & Developer Platforms)

For enterprise infrastructure, developer payment gateways (e.g., Stripe), and spend management tools, ratings are sourced from verified B2B software directories.

C. Timestamped Audits

All scores display their explicit platform source and review count benchmark (Audited August 2026) directly on the tool cards and comparison matrices.

2. UK Compliance & Practical Suitability

Beyond customer sentiment, we evaluate each software provider against core British regulatory frameworks:

  • HMRC Making Tax Digital (MTD): Verification of compatible digital VAT and Self Assessment filing.
  • FSCS Protection: Clear identification of ring-fenced bank accounts vs. electronic money institutions (EMIs).
  • RTI & CIS Compliance: Support for Real Time Information payroll and Construction Industry Scheme deductions.

3. Disclaimer & Commercial Transparency

FinStack is an independent software directory and does not provide financial, legal, or tax advice. Some links may generate partner compensation at zero additional cost to the user, but partnerships never alter our verified sourcing data.
