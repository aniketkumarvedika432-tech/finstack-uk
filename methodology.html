Trust & methodology

How FinStack evaluates tools.

We aim to make software discovery clearer for UK SMEs without presenting directory content as financial, tax or legal advice.

01

UK relevance

We prioritise providers that serve UK businesses and organise them around UK SME use cases.

02

Use-case fit

We classify tools by the business problem they can help address rather than by brand popularity alone.

03

Commercial clarity

Pricing, eligibility, features and regulatory claims should be checked against the provider before publication.

04

Independence

Potential affiliate or sponsored relationships should be disclosed and should not be presented as impartial rankings.

Important: FinStack is a software discovery directory. It does not provide financial, tax or legal advice. Provider terms, pricing, eligibility, regulation and product features can change.
